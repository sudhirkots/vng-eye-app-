Muscle courses — orbits viewed from above

Same orientation as the canal diagram (patient's LEFT on your left). The rectus muscle plane sits ~23° from the optical axis; the oblique tendons run ~51° to it — this is why SR/IR are not pure vertical movers and why the obliques are the chief torters. Dashed = passes beneath the globe or under another muscle.

Anatomical actions vs clinical testing (H-pattern)

Right eye, anterior view. A muscle's anatomical action (left) is its pull acting alone; clinical testing (right) first turns the eye so one muscle's action is isolated: SR/IR are tested in ABduction, SO/IO in ADduction. Click a muscle above to highlight its arrows here.
