Load the clip; it shows the first frame. Mark the approximate iris circle of each eye you want: press on the iris centre and drag out to the iris edge to set the radius, then release. (A plain click without a drag places a default-size circle you can re-drag.) The left half of the image is the patient's RIGHT eye; the right half is the patient's LEFT eye. Single-eye is fine — mark only the clearer eye. Then Download seed and paste the block into manual_seeds.json. This circle is only a starting window for EllSeg (centre + radius); it is NOT a measurement and is not used as the iris outline.
Re-marking on track loss: if the tracer stops and asks you to re-mark at frame F, set fps, type F in frame and press Go (or step with ◀/▶), mark the iris circle there, and add the exported mark (it carries "frame": F) to that eye as a list, e.g. "R": [ {…f0…}, {…fF…} ].
