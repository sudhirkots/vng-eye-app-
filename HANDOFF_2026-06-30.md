## Method (RIT Orbit Lock) — two approved engineering choices
- **One RANSAC similarity transform, not per-point KLT.** Track many eye-region features (pyramidal
  Lucas-Kanade), fit ONE `estimateAffinePartial2D` (translate + mild rotate + mild scale), apply to the
  whole contour as a rigid body. Per-frame scale[0.90,1.11]/rot[≤8°]/translation guards → bad transform =
  `orbit_uncertain`/`orbit_lost`, never applied. *Reason: lid margins are low-texture; per-point KLT scatters.*
- **Iris masked when selecting features.** Features sampled inside the orbit box but OUTSIDE a disc at the
  current iris position. *Reason: iris moves with GAZE (nystagmus); orbit moves with HEAD — the orbit must
  not jerk with the beats.*

UNIFIED **sclera-anchored** iris fit (`fit_iris`), iterated through several failed approaches to this:

   tracked centre — the operational form of "a convex dark area protruding into the sclera";
4. **centre = its centroid; shape = ellipse covering it** (circle full / foreshortened oval), **capped
   at the held iris radius**.

**Non-obvious reasoning:** (a) whole-blob convexity FAILS — the iris is connected to the dark lashes, so

— a fixed-radius circle overshoots into the sclera, so use the dark-region centroid.
